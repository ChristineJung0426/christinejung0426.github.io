The Arctic Epoch

Tools Unreal Engine 5, Houdini
Techniques Procedural Snow Generator, Icicle Generator, Custom Shaders
About

Procedural snow and icicle systems inside a moody dystopian cityscape

The Arctic Epoch is an environment tech project focused on building procedural systems for winter environments. A Houdini-based snow generator and icicle generator drive the look of a dystopian frozen city, with custom shaders handling material response to snow accumulation.
