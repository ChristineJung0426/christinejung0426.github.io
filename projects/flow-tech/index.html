About

Procedural tools that generate dynamic natural environments at scale

FLOW is a procedural natural assets generator built in Houdini and deployed in Unreal Engine 5. The system generates dynamic environmental effects — foliage, atmospheric particles, and weather systems — that can be iterated quickly and applied at scene scale.

This was also the technical art component of the FLOW Interactive Immersive Experience, providing the procedural world-building tools used to populate the 270-degree immersive environment.
