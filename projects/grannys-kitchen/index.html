About

A warm, lived-in kitchen built to feel nostalgic and real

Granny's Baking Kitchen is inspired by a grandmother who loves baking. The space is designed to feel cozy and nostalgic, with props and details that capture the charm of everyday baking. Every asset — from the hand-painted ceramic tiles to the worn wooden table — was crafted to tell a story.

Developed with professional feedback and guidance from Sarah Swenson, Senior Environment Artist at Naughty Dog. Plants from Quixel.
