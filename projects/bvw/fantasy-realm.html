About

Two worlds, one choice — a dream-like VR experience built in 2 weeks

You are a person who is tired of working, and you have been pulled into a fantastical realm. After exploring the new world, it is now up to you to determine whether or not to lure the next person.

Responsible for designing and building the 3D environments for both worlds — a dull, mundane office and a vivid dream-like fantasy world with landscapes, trees, and waterfall effects.

3D Environment VR Unity Maya Substance Painter
Office World

Designed a dull, corporate environment to convey a sense of monotony and exhaustion. Created the computer, table and tabletop objects, and bookshelf from scratch to build out the space.

Fantasy World

Designed multiple distinct stages within the fantastical realm, building 3D landscapes, stylized trees, and dynamic waterfall effects — all crafted to feel dream-like and otherworldly in contrast to the office.
