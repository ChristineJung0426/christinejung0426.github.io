High-fidelity props with meticulous surface storytelling

A collection of hero props demonstrating careful surface treatment — from aged, tarnished silverware that suggests decades of use, to iridescent plastic toy pieces with complex multi-layer material behavior. Each prop is built to read at close range inside an environment.
